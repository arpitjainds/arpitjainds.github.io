Abstract

The concept of Emotional Quotient (EQ) and soft-skills is extremely dynamic and abstract. The very idea of soft-skills is close to the integral feelings associated with our human nature. All living beings are composed of two closely knitted entities - Metaphysical (Spiritual) Self and Physiological Self. Metaphysical self is intangible. Physiological is the tangible counterpart. The concept of metaphysical self is extremely essential to understand the concept of soft-skills. Soft-Skills are the basic values which are present inside everyone but can only be realized when one explores the inner (Spiritual) self. The perceived meaning of soft-skills differs from one person to other but the underlying basis is identical. Through eons of time, Women have been suppressed. Most of the early societies were patriarchies. Men had the Controlling authority everywhere (Including families). This was the major reason responsible for women not being able to exert their importance as actively as men. Education for women was never given much of attention or thought. This is the reason for the lack of the number of Female Leaders and Role Models. It is only now that the World, though slowly, but is beginning to accept importance of female workers in the corporate sector. The positive and influential effect of the presence of the women in any workspace is indispensable and extremely essential for the growth of corporate sector in entirety. Men and Women are both equally capable at the spiritual level. The only bifurcation is introduced at the physiological level.

Research

Gender representation study in working India
The opportunity to work in the corporate sector has been limited and regressive for women in India on various scales. There is not just one reason but many due to which women participation in the working spaces has been negligible in India. The statistics regarding the same are dismal and disappointing. When it comes to being a part of the active workforce, only 23.7 percent of eligible Indian women are part of the workforce in India which is a sad state of affairs for the section when it is compared that to 75 percent of men who get to be part of the active workforce. The problem increases in the urban areas, as this ratio drops even further to only 16 percent of women working. The comparable number is 69 percent in men in the urban areas wherein they have more job opportunities and better pay, specifically highlighting the role of corporate sector.

In the urban areas, even for the women who get to be a part of the workforce, the unemployment rate is high. It is escalated to 8.7 percent as compared to the unemployment rate which rests at 4 percent for men. This is exactly why India ranks abysmally low in terms of participation of women in the working spaces and producing economic results. In the Global Gender Report 2015 which is released by the World Economic Forum ranked India at 139 out of 145 countries. Countries such as Syria, Saudi Arabia, Yemen and Pakistan are among the only few countries that rank below India in this survey. This problem is so critical that the same was acknowledged in the fifth annual Employment-Unemployment Survey published in September 2016, the results of the survey clearly highlighted that encouraging more women and making it an welcoming environment for them to join the workforce is critical to any nation’s ability to utilise and reap beneficial results from the demographic dividend of the nation and hence, it becomes even more important for a developing country like India to encash the same.

It has been observed that between the second employment-unemployment survey, which was conducted in 2011-12 and then the latest one which was conducted in 2015-16, participation of women in the labour force of both the rural and urban sectors has declined sharply and unemployment has risen for them.

It a commonly known fact that as education levels has risen in women along with their aspirations, many are willing to contribute in the labour force for the development of the working classes, however, the corporate industry is at a fault to not understand the advantages of having a diverse working population and the benefits of taking up women in the workforce. Factors such as lack of opportunities, unfair pay, unsafe environment to work are keeping the women away from securing jobs and due to which their emotional intelligence skills remain unused and underdeveloped.

Solution

Ways of increasing women representation in the corporate sector
As has been clearly established that women possess a higher quotient of emotional intelligence, it is absolutely necessary that measures are being taken to increase their participation in the corporate working spaces for the betterment of the industries as well as a uniform growth of the nation. There are many innovative ways through which we can eliminate the reasons which are keeping away women from engaging themselves in the working prospects and create a better and inclusive work environment from them in which they can make use of their emotional intelligence skills to the maximum potential for the purpose of reaping benefits. Following are some of the major procedures that can be adopted-

1. Equal pay for equal work- Women's work and contribution to the working space is undermined and discouraged in a way that their work is disrespected by not reciprocating it with the monetary benefits that they deserve. Distribution of wages in the corporate sector must be on the basis of the Work keeping other biased factors negligible. Hence, special attention must be paid to giving out equal wages to women and men if they're equal at work.

2. Women at Leadership Positions- Emotional Intelligence quotient is highly necessary and should be put to use in positions that require leadership qualities and working with a diverse team. Hence, adding on women will not only add on the values of compassion, understanding, cooperation and teamwork in the corporate spaces, it will also enhance the work experience of their male counterparts wherein they will learn about different techniques of approaching the same goal.

3. Diverse Employment Opportunities- Openings for women empowerment must be present in each and every strata of an organisation. Biased induction should be completely eliminated and women entrees should be made according to their educational qualifications only. Presence of a counseling center in every office is a must wherein women psychologists will find an attractive option to fit in as women can put to use their emotional intelligence skills in these fields for maximum utilisation.

4. Making Organisations Adaptable to Women's Needs- Although in the previous years, the number of women that are going for higher education degrees and are entering the workforce and being an active part of it have increased exponentially, a major portion of this population often remains suppressed in junior to sub management levels in their offices. There are many reasons that can be held responsible for this including an institutionalized bias and primarily a lack of support and cooperation for women employees who decide to bear children.The ramifications of the same is that despite being bright, worthy and talented, not many women are able to make it to the top management slots in their companies. Unfortunately, the organizations that we are a part of are naturally designed to work in male-oriented environments and set ups, and due to this only, these considerations are categorised as insignificant or even as a hurdle or an extra burden on the cooperation or a deterrent to progress of the company. However, it is essential to incorporate greater flexibility and an inclusive approach to make the workplace a much more comfortable place for women.

Reflections

As has been conclusively established in this research article that the emotional intelligence quotient of women remains unexplored and unutilised for in the corporate sector, it is the need of the hour to highlight the ways through which we can increase women participation in the corporate sector. Abilities unique to women can boost the productivity of corporate sectors in exponential rates. The sooner we realize this and let the women move out of homes to pursue careers in the professional world, the sooner we will move towards the world that is more sustainable and healthy to live in. Professor Ervin Laszlo in his Speech at TEDxNavigli (2013) about a New Love Declaration, concluded by saying that in order to move towards a more sustainable world, the power to change is more in Women as compared to Men. Once the Cycle of suppression is terminated and the power of expression is handed over to the women of the society, we’ll achieve Utopian era of excellence.
